Jollof Rice Recipie

This is recipie is prepared according to the way Ghanaians typically make it.

Ingredients

  • Onions
  • Spring onions
  • Green Bell pepper
  • Tomato sauce
  • Habanero peppers
  • Tomatoes
  • Dawadawa powder
  • Nketenkete
  • Curry powder
  • Salt
  • Rice
  • Olive Oil
  • Rosemary powder

Instructions

  1. Pour some Olive oil into the pot, just a little over enough to grease it.
  2. When the oil is heated up add a sliced up onion and let the onions saute
  3. After they have sauteed for a few minutes, add half teaspoon of Rosemary powder
  4. Blend a green bell pepper, some spring onions and an onion
  5. Add the blended mixture to the onions, rosemary and oil
